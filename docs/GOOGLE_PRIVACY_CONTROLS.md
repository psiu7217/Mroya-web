# Google data controls

The live Connections screen shows the settings when the Backend advertises
`google_data_privacy`. Consent is versioned by the Backend. The Web sends
consent and privacy requests through its session and CSRF transport.

A deletion receipt confirms intake for operator review. A Calendar disconnect
reports confirmed revocation, an outstanding retry, or an unavailable
confirmation from the Backend. Disconnect and AI consent have independent
controls.

Use the linked Privacy Policy, Terms and support channel to explain the
applicable process. The Backend retains the operator release gate for
provider routing, retention and erasure verification.
